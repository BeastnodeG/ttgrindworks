Installation:
Windows/Linux: Create a "mods" folder next to your "ToontownTheGrindworks.exe" and place the desired mod(s) .zip into it. (Do not extract the zip)
MacOS: Create a "mods" folder in the /Applications folder and the desired mod(s) .zip into it. (Do not extract the zip)

Changes:
Green Folio - ~"The Sewers"~ "*The* Green Folio" (The Folio That Might Be Green)
- The Green Folio
	- "Wahhh the games too easy, oh yeah???"
	- Adds 10 Folio modifiers you can add upon starting a run.
		- By default only 1 of these modifiers is unlocked. You unlock the next modifier by completing the next highest avaliable Folio.
		- All Folio modifiers stack upon one and another, and are displayed via a permanent anomaly.
- The Sewers
	- The Sewers can now be visited alongside the other floors in the game!
	- This floor is a little bit harder than the other floors, and has no environmental end boss.
